# Motorola Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** created to analyze Motorola mobile sales performance across cities, brands, mobile models, days, customer ratings, and payment methods.

The dashboard converts transactional sales data into an easy-to-understand visual report with KPI cards, filters, charts, a city map, and summary tables.

## Dashboard Preview

![Motorola Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Track total sales, quantity, transactions, and average sales.
- Compare sales performance across brands and mobile models.
- Understand sales distribution across cities.
- Analyze daily sales and quantity trends.
- Understand customer rating distribution.
- Analyze transactions by payment method.
- Provide interactive filters for business-level analysis.

## Dashboard Components

### 1. KPI Cards
Created summary cards for:
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Transactions
- Average Sales

These provide a quick overview of the selected data.

### 2. Interactive Filters
Added slicers for:
- Mobile Model
- Payment Method
- Brand
- Day Name
- Month

These filters allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze specific segments.

### 3. Total Sales by City
Used a geographic map to visualize sales across different cities in India, making it easier to identify where sales are distributed geographically.

### 4. Total Quantity by Day
Created a line chart to show how the quantity sold changes across the days of the month.

### 5. Customer Ratings
Created a horizontal bar chart to show the number of transactions/customers associated with each rating level.

### 6. Transactions by Payment Method
Created a donut/pie chart to analyze transactions by:
- Debit Card
- Cash
- UPI
- Credit Card

### 7. Brand Summary
Created a summary table containing:
- Brand
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Transactions

The dashboard compares brands such as Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, and Xiaomi.

### 8. Total Sales by Mobile Model
Created a bar chart to compare sales across mobile models, including models such as Vivo S1, Galaxy A51, and iPhone SE.

### 9. Total Sales by Day Name
Created an area/line chart to compare sales performance across weekdays.

## Business Insights Supported by the Dashboard
The dashboard structure allows users to investigate questions such as:
- Which brand generates the highest sales?
- Which mobile model contributes the most sales?
- Which cities have higher sales activity?
- Which payment method is used most frequently?
- Which days have higher or lower sales?
- How are customer ratings distributed?
- How do sales and quantity change when a brand, model, month, or payment method is selected?

## Dashboard Design
The dashboard was designed as a single-page interactive report with:
- KPI cards at the top
- Slicers below the KPIs
- Geographic analysis on the left
- Trend and customer analysis in the center
- Payment analysis on the right
- Brand/model/day summaries at the bottom
